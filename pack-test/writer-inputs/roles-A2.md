# Fourteen more ideas to turn into concrete events (set A2, two events each = 28 items)

Same instructions as the other ideas file: for each idea, write TWO different concrete events that illustrate it, with actors and objects, in everyday words.

1. announce-interest: A device announces which part of the shared space it wants to receive.
2. record-a-look: Recording that a particular device looked at a particular item.
3. track-popularity: Keeping track of which types of items get looked at most, to decide what to keep nearby when space is short.
4. start-a-space: Creating a new, empty shared space, or a new person's own area inside one.
5. make-a-group: Creating a new group of people or devices, or installing a ready-made bundle of item types.
6. stop-holding: A device stops holding everything in one area of the shared space, without removing it for anyone else.
7. remove-a-whole-branch: Removing a whole folder and everything under it in one step, or withdrawing a permission together with every permission that was passed on from it.
8. inherit-or-same-person: Making a new item inherit the permissions of the folder it sits in, or declaring that two separate spaces contain the same person.
9. work-out-the-chain: Working out the chain of who can reach what, and through whom.
10. merge-two-spaces: Merging two separate shared spaces into one.
11. merge-two-versions: Combining two conflicting versions of the same item into one.
12. merge-permission-records: Combining two devices' records of who has permission over what.
13. change-one-secret: Replacing the secret code protecting one item with a new one.
14. change-group-secret: Replacing the shared secret code of a whole group after someone leaves, or switching a group to a new version of its rule set.
